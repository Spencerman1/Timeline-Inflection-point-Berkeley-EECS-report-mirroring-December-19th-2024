### **Origins of the Naming**

* **Philosophical roots: The word *agentic* comes from *agency* in philosophy and psychology — the capacity to act independently and purposefully [mljourney.com](https://mljourney.com/why-is-agentic-ai-called-agentic/).**  
* **AI evolution: As large language models (LLMs) matured, developers began building systems that could set goals, plan, and act autonomously rather than just generate text. This shift prompted the need for a new label.**  
* **First usage in AI: Around 2023, researchers and industry blogs started using “agentic AI” to describe these autonomous agents. By 2024, the term was widely adopted in academic papers, corporate strategy documents, and platforms like IBM and Google Cloud [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/agentic-ai) [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/discover/what-is-agentic-ai).**  
* **Popularization: By 2025, “agentic AI” had become a mainstream descriptor for AI agents that integrate memory, orchestration, and decision‑making capabilities [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agentic_AI) [Dun & Bradstreet](https://www.dnb.com/en-us/resources/ai/what-is-agentic-ai-definition-meaning-and-how-ai-agents-work.html).**

---

### **Why the Naming Matters**

* **Differentiation: It separates autonomous agents from traditional generative AI, emphasizing decision‑making over content creation.**  
* **Governance implications: The term highlights the need for ethical frameworks and protocols (like Loka Protocol) to manage autonomous systems.**  
* **Industry adoption: Companies like IBM and Google Cloud now use “agentic AI” in official materials, signaling its acceptance as a technical category.**

---

### **Timeline Summary**

* **Pre‑2023: AI agents existed but were usually called “multi‑agent systems” or “intelligent agents.”**  
* **2023: Early references to “agentic AI” emerge in research and commentary.**  
* **2024: Term gains traction in academic and industry contexts.**  
* **2025: Widely adopted in mainstream AI discourse, with dedicated definitions and strategy guides.**
